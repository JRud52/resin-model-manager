            MyMiniFactory library

            MyMiniFactory has no way for other apps to read your library, so a bookmark does it from your own logged-in browser. Your purchases, pledges, tribes, groups, MMF+ and free downloads are listed here with a link to each one; nothing is downloaded and your MyMiniFactory login is never stored.

            1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Sync to Resin Models
            2. Go to myminifactory.com and log in.
            3. Click the bookmark. A Resin Models window opens and fills in your library. Click it again whenever you buy or pledge something new.

            Make the bookmark from this page at the address you normally use for Resin Models; it sends the list back to that address.

            The upload is for when the bookmark can't open the Resin Models window: it saves that file instead.
